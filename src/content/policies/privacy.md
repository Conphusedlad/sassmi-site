## What we collect

- **When you order:** your name, email address, mobile number, shipping address, the items you bought and the amount paid. Card, UPI and bank details are entered directly with **Razorpay**, our payment processor — we never see or store them.
- **When you write to us:** your name, email, phone number (optional) and message.
- **When you leave a review:** your name and the words you write.
- **When you subscribe:** your email address.
- **Automatically:** basic technical data such as your device type, browser and pages visited, used only to keep the site working and to understand what people read.

## Why we use it

- To process, pack, ship and support your order, and to send you the receipt and delivery updates.
- To reply to your questions and complaints and to keep a record of them (ticket numbers).
- To publish reviews you have chosen to post.
- To send our newsletter — only if you subscribed, and you can leave with one click.
- To meet legal obligations (tax invoices, food-safety traceability, consumer-protection records).

We do **not** sell or rent your data. We do not use it for profiling or targeted advertising.

## Who we share it with

- **Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd.** — to take payment (governed by their privacy policy).
- **Courier partners** — your name, address and phone number, so the parcel reaches you.
- **Email service providers** — to send order and support emails.
- **Authorities** — where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for eight years as required by tax and food-safety law. Support tickets are kept for three years. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Reviews stay published until you ask us to remove them.
