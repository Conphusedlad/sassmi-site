## Timelines

- **Dispatch:** within 1–2 working days of payment. We roast in small batches, so occasionally a flavour may need an extra day — we will tell you.
- **Delivery:** usually **{{ETA}}** after dispatch for metro cities; remote PIN codes can take longer. Delhi NCR orders often arrive in 2–3 days.
- Working days exclude Sundays and public holidays.

## Tracking

You will receive the courier name and tracking number by email (and WhatsApp where you have shared a number) when your parcel leaves us.

## Failed delivery and wrong address

Please check your address and phone number carefully at checkout — couriers call before delivery. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incomplete or nobody was available after two attempts, we will contact you to re-ship; a re-shipping charge{{RESHIP_FEE}} may apply.

## Damaged in transit

If the outer box arrives visibly damaged, please photograph it before opening and see our Refund & Cancellation Policy — we replace damaged tins free of charge.
