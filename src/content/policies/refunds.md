We want every tin to arrive perfect. Because makhana is a food product, we cannot accept returns of opened food — but we make things right when something goes wrong.

## Cancellation

- You may cancel an order **any time before it is dispatched** by emailing {{EMAIL}} or messaging {{PHONE}} with your order number. The full amount is refunded.
- Once the parcel has left us, the order cannot be cancelled; please see the sections below.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your tin arrives damaged, the seal is broken, the product is defective, or you received the wrong flavour or quantity:

1. Write to us within **7 days of delivery** at {{EMAIL}} or {{PHONE}} with your order number.
2. Attach photos of the outer box, the tin and the product (an unboxing photo or video helps us settle claims instantly).
3. We will send a **free replacement** or issue a **full refund** for the affected items — your choice — within 2 working days of verifying the claim. No need to ship anything back.

## Refund method and timeline

Refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay and typically reflect in **5–7 working days**, depending on your bank. We will email you when the refund is initiated.

## What we cannot refund

- Opened or partly consumed tins where the product is not defective.
- Taste preferences — flavour descriptions and heat levels are given on each product page to help you choose.
- Delays caused by incorrect address details or the customer being unavailable.

## Failed payments

If your payment failed but money was debited, it is normally auto-reversed by your bank within 5–7 working days. Send us the payment ID and we will follow up with Razorpay for you.
